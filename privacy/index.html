Privacy notice

Skelpr is published by Thrive360 and Hashim Kalam. This notice covers skelpr.com, the release-updates waitlist and email correspondence with Skelpr.

What we collect

  • Waitlist. When you sign up, we store your email address, signup time and follow-up status in Google Sheets via Google Apps Script. We use the list to send Skelpr release updates. Entries do not expire automatically.
  • Access requests and support. When you email us, we use your address and the details you provide to reply, manage access or troubleshoot the issue. We keep correspondence as needed for those purposes.
  • Site visits. Vercel processes requests to serve the site. We use Vercel Web Analytics to count visits. It can process the page URL, referrer, approximate location, browser and device details. Analytics uses a temporary visitor hash instead of a tracking cookie.
  • Theme. The site saves your selected theme in your browser's local storage so it can remember it on your next visit.

Unsubscribe or contact us

Email theskelpr@gmail.com to stop release updates or request a copy, correction or deletion of your information. For waitlist requests, email us from the address you signed up with.
